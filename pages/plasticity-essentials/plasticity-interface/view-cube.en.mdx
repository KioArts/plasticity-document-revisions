# View Cube

## View Cube

The **3D View Cube** in Plasticity serves as a visual and interactive reference for orientation in the 3D workspace. It provides users with a quick way to understand and manipulate the viewing direction of their model.

{/* ![View Cube](/img/view-cube.png) */}
![View Cube Interaction](/img/view-cube-arrows.svg)

## Features

- **Axis Indicators:** The cube displays three labeled axes:

        - <ButtonIcon type="x-arrow"/> **X-axis** / <ButtonIcon type="y-arrow"/> **Y-axis** / <ButtonIcon type="z-arrow"/> **Z-axis**

**View Manipulation:** Clicking on different faces and edges of the cube changes the camera view to predefined orientations.

## Interaction

- **Click on an Axis arrow**: Aligns the camera to view in that axis direction.
    - **X-axis**: Aligns the camera in Right view.
    - **Y-axis**: Aligns the camera in Back view.
    - **Z-Axis**: Aligns the camera in Top view.

{/* ![View Cube Interaction](/img/view-cube-arrows.svg) */}

<br/>

- **Click on a Face**: Aligns the camera to that face. Double-clicking flips the view.


![View Cube Interaction](/img/view-cube-faces.svg)

<br/>

- **Click on an Edge**: Click on the cube's edges to view the model from an isometric perspective.

![View Cube Interaction](/img/view-cube-edges.svg)

<br/>

- **Click on a Corner**: Click on the cube's corners to view the model from an isometric perspective.

![View Cube Interaction](/img/view-cube-corners.svg)

<br/>

- **Right-Click on the View Cube**: Opens a context menu of viewport view commands, with their shortcuts.

![Context Menu View Cube](/img/context-menu-view-cube.png)
