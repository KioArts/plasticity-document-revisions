## Edge Align

The **Edge Align** command runs based on the selected object:
     - **Surface Align:** If two Surface Edges are selected, see [**Surface Align**](/solid/surface-align).
     - **Vertex Align:** If two Vertices or Curves are selected, see [**Vertex Align**](/sketch/vertex-align) in the Sketch Commands section.
