## Edge Align

The **Edge Align** command runs based on the selected object:
     - **Vertex Align:** If two Vertices or Curves are selected, see [**Vertex Align**](vertex-align).
     - **Surface Align:** If two Edges are selected, see [**Surface Align**](/solid/surface-align) in the Solid Commands section.
